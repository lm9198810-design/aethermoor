# ◈ AETHERMOOR
### The Sundered Realm — A Fantasy World Chronicle

Where gods have fallen and left their thrones empty.

---

## About
An interactive world chronicle of Aethermoor — a fantasy realm of seven races, five schools of magic, twelve great houses, and a thousand years of ash.

Built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Live Site
🔗 https://lm9198810-design.github.io/aethermoor/

## The World
- **7 Races** — Aeldrin, Draekon, Karathi, Vordrim, Sylvaar, Kemiiri, Halfborn
- **5 Schools of Magic** — Breathweaving, Runecraft, Bloodfire, Voidwalking, Song of Stars
- **12 Great Houses** — From House Vaelor to House Skycaller
- **15 Regions** — From the Silver Wood to the Dead Marches
- **1000 Years of History** — From the Sundering to the Present Day
